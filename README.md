# JJ's Memory Match

A bright, friendly memory matching game made for JJ — a 7-year-old playing on a phone. Built with Kiro.

## What the game is

Flip over cards two at a time and find the matching pairs. The cards hide fun **gaming emoji** — game controllers, consoles, and arcade/retro gaming symbols like 🎮 🕹️ 👾 🎲 🏆 📺. The emoji are picked at random every game, so it's a little different each time.

It's a full **multi-level** adventure: there are **5 levels** that get bigger and trickier, you earn **coins** for every match (and a bonus for finishing quickly), you can tap a **Peek** button for a quick hint, and you can spend your coins in the **Shop** on new card faces, card backs, and confetti — then equip them from the **Rewards** screen. Everything you earn is saved under your name, and the whole game still works **completely offline**. Try to find all the pairs in as few moves as you can!

## Levels

The game has **5 levels** that get a little bigger and trickier as you go. Each level fits neatly on a phone screen, with the cards shrinking just enough so a full board always fits with no scrolling.

| Level | Pairs | Cards | Grid |
|-------|-------|-------|------|
| 1     | 6     | 12    | 3 × 4 |
| 2     | 8     | 16    | 4 × 4 |
| 3     | 10    | 20    | 4 × 5 |
| 4     | 12    | 24    | 4 × 6 |
| 5     | 15    | 30    | 5 × 6 |

- **Level select screen.** After you tap **Play!**, you pick a level from a list that shows each level's number, how many pairs it has, and your **best (fewest) moves** for that level.
- **Locked and unlocked.** You start with **Level 1 unlocked** and the rest **locked** (🔒). **Finishing a level unlocks the next one.** Locked levels can't be started until you earn them.
- **Saved per player.** Which levels you've unlocked and your best moves for each level are saved under your name, so every player builds up their own progress.

## Coins

You earn **coins** 🪙 just by playing — there are **no real-money purchases anywhere**. Coins are the only way to unlock the Peek hint and the goodies in the Shop, and coins are **saved under your name** so your stash is always there when you come back.

How coins are earned:

- **Every matched pair gives you 5 coins.** The more pairs you find, the more you earn.
- **Finish a level and get a bonus for being quick.** Each level has a "par" of `pairs × 2` moves (the expected number of tries). You always get a flat **5-coin clear bonus**, plus **2 extra coins for every move you finished *under* par**.
  - So the finish bonus is `5 + (moves under par) × 2`. Finishing right at or over par still gives the flat 5 — you can never lose coins.
  - *Example:* a perfect Level 1 (6 pairs found in just 6 moves) earns **30** coins for the matches plus a **17**-coin bonus (`5 + 6 × 2`) — **47 coins** in one go!

Your coin total is shown on the start screen (for whoever's name is in the box) and on a little 🪙 pill while you play.

## Peek (hints)

Stuck? During a game there's a **Peek 👀** button. Tap it and **all the cards flip face-up for 2 seconds** so you can take a quick look, then the unmatched ones flip back. Each Peek **costs coins** — the price is shown right on the button (e.g. `Peek 👀 (10🪙)`). If you don't have enough coins the button is greyed out until you earn more, and it also pauses while cards are flipping so it never messes up your turn.

## Shop

Open the **🛒 Shop** from the start screen or the level select screen to spend your coins on fun extras. Everything in the Shop is bought with **coins you earned by playing — never real money.** Items you can buy:

- **Card faces** — swap the emoji on the cards for a whole new set: **Space** 🚀, **Food** 🍕, **Animals** 🐶, or **Sea** 🐠. (The original **Gaming** 🎮 set is free and always yours.)
- **Card backs** — new looks for the back of every card: **Ocean** 🌊, **Galaxy** ✨, **Candy** 🍭, or **Jungle** 🍃. (The classic pink ★ back is free.)
- **Confetti** — change the celebration burst when you win: **Hearts** ❤️, **Stars** 🌟, or **Sea** 🐠. (The classic burst is free.)

Each item shows a preview and its price. The **Buy** button is disabled if you can't afford it yet, and anything you already own is marked **✔ Owned**. Buying something spends the coins straight away and the item is saved under your name forever.

## Rewards

Open the **🎁 Rewards** screen to **equip** the things you own. For each kind of reward (card faces, card backs, confetti) it lists everything in your collection and lets you pick which one to use — just tap **Equip**. The one you're using is clearly marked **✔ Equipped**.

- Equipping a **card face** changes which emoji the cards use in your next game.
- Equipping a **card back** changes the design on the back of the cards.
- Equipping a **confetti** style changes the burst you see when you win.

Your choices are saved under your name, so each player can have their own favourite look. A brand-new player simply starts with the free Gaming faces, classic card back, and classic confetti.

## How to play

1. Type your name in the big friendly name box on the start screen, then tap **Play!**
   - Your name is remembered for next time, so you don't have to retype it.
2. Pick a level from the **level select** screen (locked levels unlock as you finish earlier ones). From here you can also jump to the **🛒 Shop** or **🎁 Rewards**, or tap **Back** to return to the start screen.
3. Tap a card to flip it over (it gives a happy little pop and squish). Stuck? Tap **Peek 👀** to see all the cards for 2 seconds (it costs a few coins).
4. Tap a second card.
   - **Match!** 🎉 The two cards stay face-up with a happy green glow and a cheerful sound.
   - **No match?** The cards flip back over after a moment — remember where they were!
5. Keep going until all the pairs in the level are found. You earn **coins** for every match along the way.
6. Watch the **Moves** counter and your 🪙 coin pill at the top. The **⬅ Levels** button takes you back to the level select screen any time.
7. When you find every pair, a celebration banner and a burst of confetti/stars show your final move count **and the coins you earned** (match coins plus a fewer-moves bonus). From there you can tap **Next Level** (once it's unlocked), **Play Again** to reshuffle the same level, **Level Select** to pick another level, or **Leaderboard** to see the best scores.
8. Spend your coins in the **🛒 Shop** on new card faces, card backs, and confetti, then **🎁 equip** them from the Rewards screen to make the game your own.

## Leaderboard

The game keeps a **Leaderboard** of the top 10 games, **fewest moves first**. You can open it from the start screen or from the win banner. Each entry shows the player's name, their move count (the moves used to finish that level), and the date.

- **Saved on the device.** Every finished game is saved right on the phone or computer using the browser's storage, so the board stays even after you close and reopen the game. Different players on the same device each appear under their own name.
- **Online family leaderboard.** When there's an internet connection, the Leaderboard screen also shows a shared **family** board so everyone's best games can compete across devices. Finished scores are submitted quietly in the background.
- **Offline fallback.** No internet? No problem. If the family server can't be reached, the game instantly falls back to the scores saved on the device and keeps working perfectly. Gameplay is never blocked or interrupted by the network.
- **Clear board.** On the Leaderboard screen there's a small **Clear board** option (with a "are you sure?" confirm step) so the family can reset the device's saved scores.

## Sound

Happy sound effects play as you tap, match, and win — all made with the browser's built-in Web Audio (no sound files to download). There's a **🔊 / 🔇 sound toggle** in the top-right corner; sound is **on by default** and your choice is remembered.

## Look and feel

A gentle animated background of drifting gaming shapes and stars sets the arcade mood without distracting from play. Cards flip, bounce, and pop, matched pairs celebrate, buttons squish when pressed, and winning sets off a confetti/star burst.

## How to open it

Just open **`index.html`** in any web browser — double-click the file, or drag it into a browser window. On a phone, open the file in your mobile browser.

That's it. No installation and no setup needed — the whole game lives in a single file. It works **completely offline**; the only time it reaches the internet is to share scores on the optional family leaderboard, and it falls back gracefully to the device's saved scores whenever there's no connection.
